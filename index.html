THE METHOD

Evidence before novelty.

Delver treats a card as archetype core when it appears in at least two thirds of the sampled lists. Everything else is a flex slot. Test ideas come from successful lists that use a coherent alternative package, then suggest a same-role cut from your own list.

Delver merges recent tournament sources with small, cached archetype supplements. Treat community lists and historical imports as ideas, not metagame proof.
